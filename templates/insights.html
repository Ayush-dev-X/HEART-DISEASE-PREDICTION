06 · MODEL INSIGHTS

What the Model Evaluation Shows

Key observations directly derived from the experiments performed in the notebook.

01 PERFORMANCE

Logistic Regression

Recorded the highest test accuracy among the five evaluated models at 86.41%, with an F1 score of 87.92%.

02 FEATURE SPACE

15 Model Inputs

One-hot encoding expands the original 12-column dataset into a 15-feature input space used by the trained classifier.

03 SCALING

Standardized Inputs

Numerical model inputs are standardized with StandardScaler before reaching the trained model.

04 DATA BALANCE

Moderately Imbalanced

The target contains 508 positive and 410 negative observations, giving a 55.34% / 44.66% distribution.
